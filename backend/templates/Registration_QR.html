💡 Tips to Protect Yourself
🔐 Verify the QR code source
Only scan QR codes from trusted merchants or official platforms. Be cautious if a QR code appears pasted over another one or placed in suspicious locations.
💰 Check the payment details before confirming
After scanning a QR code, always verify the receiver’s name, UPI ID, or merchant details before completing the transaction.
🔗 Avoid random QR codes shared via messages
Do not scan QR codes received through unknown emails, social media, or messaging apps claiming prizes, refunds, or urgent payments.
🛑 Remember: QR codes cannot "receive" money
If someone asks you to scan a QR code to receive money, it is almost always a scam. Scanning a payment QR usually means you are sending money.
⚠️ Watch for tampered QR stickers
Fraudsters sometimes place fake QR stickers over legitimate ones at shops, parking meters, or public places.
🔍 Confirm with the merchant if unsure
If you feel suspicious, ask the merchant to show the original payment QR or verify through their official payment method.
🛡️ Use secure payment apps and alerts
Enable transaction alerts and payment notifications so you can quickly detect unauthorized transactions.
