Tips to Protect Yourself

  • 🔐
    Download from trusted sources only

    Always download software or apps from official websites or trusted app stores. Avoid downloading files from unknown or suspicious websites.

  • 🔗
    Be cautious with email attachments

    Do not open attachments or download files from emails sent by unknown or suspicious senders.

  • 🛑
    Avoid pirated or cracked software

    Free versions of paid software from unofficial sites often contain hidden malware that can infect your system.

  • ⚠️
    Watch for fake download buttons

    Some websites display fake download buttons that install malware instead of the intended file. Always verify the correct download link.

  • 🔍
    Check file types before downloading

    Be cautious with files ending in .exe, .zip, .apk, or .scr, especially if they come from untrusted sources.

  • 🛡️
    Use antivirus and keep systems updated

    Install reliable antivirus software and regularly update your operating system and applications to protect against new threats.

Being cautious online is the best defense against phishing. Always think before you click, verify messages, and protect your personal information.
