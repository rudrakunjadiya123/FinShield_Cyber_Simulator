⚠️ Phishing Awareness ⚠️

This is a Phishing simulation successfully implemented on you. Relax, this is for experimental awareness. Tips to Protect Yourself:

🔐 Check the sender carefully
Always verify the email address or phone number. Phishers often use addresses that look similar to legitimate ones.

🔗 Do not click suspicious links
Avoid clicking links or downloading attachments from unknown or unexpected messages. Hover over links to check the real URL before clicking.

🛑 Never share sensitive information
Banks, companies, or government agencies will never ask for passwords, OTPs, or confidential details through email or messages.

⚠️ Look for warning signs
Phishing messages often contain spelling mistakes, urgent language (like “Act now!”), or requests for immediate action.

🔍 Verify with the official source
If you receive a suspicious message, contact the organization directly through their official website or phone number.

🛡️ Use security tools
Install antivirus software, enable spam filters, and keep your devices updated to help detect phishing attempts.

Stay Aware

Being cautious online is the best defense against phishing. Always think before you click, verify messages, and protect your personal information.
